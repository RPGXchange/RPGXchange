- docs/static/img/branding/favicon.ico

## Permitted Use

You may use RPGXchange branding ONLY to:

1. Indicate compatibility or support for the RPGXchange file format
   Examples:
   - "Supports RPGXchange" with logo
   - "Compatible with RPGXchange" with logo
   - Including logo in documentation showing import/export functionality
   - Badges indicating format support

2. Reference the RPGXchange project/specification with proper attribution
   Examples:
   - "This tool can import RPGXchange files"
   - Linking to the official RPGXchange repository
   - "Based on the RPGXchange specification"

All uses must:

- NOT suggest ownership, endorsement, or official affiliation
- Include a clear disclaimer if needed (e.g., "RPGXchange is an independent
  format specification not affiliated with [user's project]")
- NOT modify the logo or branding assets
- Include attribution linking to https://github.com/rpgxchange/rpgxchange or https://rpgxchange.github.io/RPGXchange

## Prohibited Use

You may NOT use RPGXchange branding for:

- Naming or rebranding derivative projects or forks as RPGXchange
- Creating modified specifications and calling them RPGXchange
- Implying official affiliation, partnership, or endorsement
- Using in ways that could cause consumer confusion about ownership
- Using on competing TTRPG file format projects
- Modifying, altering, or creating derivative versions of the logo
- Claiming any intellectual property rights to the branding

## Derivative Specifications

If you create a derivative or extension of the RPGXchange specification:

- You may reference compatibility with RPGXchange using the branding
- You must NOT name your format "RPGXchange" or similar
- You must NOT use the RPGXchange logo in a way suggesting it's the
  official RPGXchange format
- Clear distinction between your format and the official spec is required

## Questions and Good Faith Use

These guidelines are meant to be clear and reasonable. Most projects want to do
the right thing—if you're unsure whether your use is permitted, just ask.

For questions about your specific use case:

- Open an issue on the GitHub repository, or
- Contact whix100 at gmail dot com

I'm generally happy to discuss and approve good-faith uses that fall outside
these guidelines.

## What This Means Legally

This branding policy establishes clear guidelines for trademark use. The
existence of these terms means:

- You cannot claim you didn't understand the restrictions
- Violations may result in requests to remove/modify branding
- Egregious violations (passing off your work as RPGXchange, trademark
  squatting, etc.) may escalate to formal cease-and-desist or DMCA notices

However, this is primarily about protecting the project and its users from
confusion—not litigation.
